How to play ✨

  1. Start at Level 1. Complete each level to unlock the next one.
  2. Draw cards from the stock and click or drag a card into a slot.
  3. Use Validate to check your arrangement. You can rearrange cards before validating.
  4. Each level has six rounds. Complete the rounds to reveal a mystery hex color, then identify it from a lineup.
  5. Every reshuffle and failed validation affects your score. Drawing cards does not cost points.

ΔE: a numerical estimate of color difference. ΔE76 and CIEDE2000 (ΔE00) are different formulas and can rank pairs differently.
